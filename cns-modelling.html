CNS modelling & neurotechnology

Make the mechanism a testable question.

I help drug-development and neurotechnology teams use EEG/MEG and generative models to investigate treatment effects and circuit dynamics.

A modelling platform for research decisions

My research software models thalamocortical and cortical population dynamics, predicts neurophysiological observations and fits model parameters through Bayesian inference. The aim is to compare mechanistic explanations of an observed effect and quantify what the data can support.

Applications include pharmacological perturbations, changes in excitation and inhibition, treatment-associated dynamics and candidate biomarker evaluation. Model-derived parameters are conditional on the model and data; they are not direct measurements of receptor density or occupancy.

A focused first pilot

1. Feasibility

Review one dataset, the acquisition and preprocessing, sample structure, intervention and outcome. Define a primary question and an analysis plan.

2. Fit & challenge

Fit a specified model and comparator. Assess parameter recovery, sensitivity, fit quality and uncertainty. Separate exploratory findings from confirmatory testing.

3. Decide

Deliver reproducible code, diagnostics and an interpretation report, with criteria for further validation or a justified decision to stop.

What you receive

A scoped analysis pipeline; documented model assumptions; fitting and uncertainty diagnostics; a comparison against agreed baselines; and a technical handover tied to your scientific decision.

Research and development services. Any use as a clinical endpoint, diagnostic or treatment-selection tool requires separate validation for its intended use.
