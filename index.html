Working together

Start with a question worth answering.

Review & scope

Clarify the question, available data and constraints. Agree what a useful result looks like and which uncertainties matter.

Build & evaluate

Develop a focused analysis or prototype, compare it with a meaningful baseline, and examine where it works and fails.

Interpret & hand over

Deliver code or analysis, explain the evidence and limitations, and define a credible next stage.

About

Dr Alexander D. Shaw

I am a computational neuroscientist and AI researcher with more than a decade of experience in generative modelling, human neurophysiology and interdisciplinary research. My work connects mechanisms in the brain with principles for adaptive intelligent systems.

I develop research software in Python, MATLAB and Julia, and have built embodied-agent prototypes in Habitat-Sim, ReplicaCAD and PyBullet. My experience includes research posts at Cambridge and Cardiff, programme leadership at Exeter, and industry-linked work in neuropharmacology.

This is my independent professional portfolio. Consulting enquiries are made directly to me in a personal capacity.
